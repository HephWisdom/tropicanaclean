# Research and asset sources

Reviewed 19 October 2026. This is an independent website concept, not an official business publication. Stock photographs are illustrative interiors, not TropicanaClean clients or completed work.

## Business facts

Primary source: [TropicanaClean Ltd on Yell](https://www.yell.com/biz/tropicanaclean-ltd-manchester-9825674/).

- Telephone: 0161 227 8297 / +44 161 227 8297.
- Address: 71 Langshaw Street, Manchester, M16 9LE, United Kingdom.
- Listing shows 5.0 from six ratings. The website attributes and links the rating to Yell; it does not describe these reviews as recent.
- General and deep cleaning, housekeeping, oven cleaning, internal windows/blinds, surface cleaning, pre/end-of-tenancy cleaning, laundry and short-term letting co-hosting are published services. The six homepage service groupings are editorial descriptions of these offerings.
- Free initial consultation is a published listing feature. No insurance, credentials, experience, pricing, guarantees or team members have been invented.
- Homepage coverage locations are explicitly named in the business description: Manchester, Salford, Stockport, Didsbury, Sale, Wilmslow, Hale and Timperley. The brief's Altrincham was replaced with Hale because Altrincham only appeared among Yell's generic nearby-search links, not the business's own description.
- Historical website https://tropicanaclean.co.uk/ was attempted but unavailable through the research browser. Yell currently links to Facebook. No existing company imagery was copied. The typographic logo and freshness motif are original concept artwork.
